# Content Editing Guide for Non-Technical Users

This guide shows you how to edit your website's content without any coding knowledge!

## 🎯 What You Can Edit

- ✏️ Site title and tagline
- 🖼️ Background slideshow images
- 🔗 Social media links (Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.)
- ⏱️ Slideshow transition speed

## 🚀 How to Access the Editor

1. Open your web browser
2. Go to: `https://YOUR_USERNAME.github.io/YOUR_PUBLIC_REPO_NAME/admin/`
3. Click **"Login with GitHub"**
4. Authorize the application when prompted
5. You'll see the content management interface!

## 📝 Editing Content

### Changing Site Title and Tagline

1. In the left sidebar, click **"Site Settings"**
2. Click **"General Settings"**
3. Edit the fields:
   - **Site Title**: The main heading on your homepage
   - **Tagline**: The subtitle below the heading
4. Click **"Save"** at the top
5. Click **"Publish"** to make it live

### Adding or Changing Slideshow Images

1. Click **"Slideshow Images"** in the sidebar
2. To **add a new slide**:
   - Scroll down
   - Click **"+ Add Slides"**
   - Click "Choose an image" to upload
   - Add a description in "Alt Text" (for accessibility)
3. To **remove a slide**:
   - Click the **X** button next to the slide
4. To **reorder slides**:
   - Drag and drop slides using the handle (⋮⋮)
5. Click **"Save"** then **"Publish"**

### Editing Social Media Links

1. Click **"Site Settings"** → **"General Settings"**
2. Scroll to **"Social Links"**
3. For each link:
   - **Name**: Display name (e.g., "Instagram")
   - **URL**: Full link (e.g., `https://instagram.com/yourusername`)
   - **Icon**: Choose the appropriate icon from dropdown
4. To **add a link**: Click **"+ Add social links"**
5. To **remove a link**: Click the **X** button
6. Click **"Save"** then **"Publish"**

### Changing Slideshow Speed

1. Click **"Site Settings"** → **"General Settings"**
2. Find **"Slideshow Transition Speed"**
3. Enter a number in milliseconds:
   - `3000` = 3 seconds (default)
   - `5000` = 5 seconds
   - `2000` = 2 seconds
4. Click **"Save"** then **"Publish"**

## 📊 Editorial Workflow

Decap CMS uses an **editorial workflow** with drafts:

1. **Draft**: Your changes are saved but not live
2. **In Review**: Ready for someone to review
3. **Ready**: Approved and ready to publish
4. **Published**: Live on the website

### Publishing Changes

After making edits:

1. Click **"Save"**
2. Your changes are in **"Draft"** status
3. Click **"Set status"** → **"Ready"**
4. Click **"Publish"** → **"Publish now"**
5. Wait 2-3 minutes for the site to rebuild

You'll see your changes live at your website URL!

## 🖼️ Image Guidelines

For best results with slideshow images:

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: 1920x1080 pixels or larger
- **File size**: Keep under 1MB for fast loading
- **Content**: Professional photos related to entrepreneurship
- **Orientation**: Landscape (horizontal)

## ⚠️ Common Issues

### "I don't see my changes"
- Did you click **"Publish"**? (Not just "Save")
- Wait 2-3 minutes for the site to rebuild
- Try refreshing your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)

### "I can't log in"
- Make sure you're using your GitHub account
- Contact your website administrator to verify access

### "My image looks blurry"
- Upload a higher resolution image (at least 1920x1080)

### "I made a mistake!"
- Don't worry! You can click **"Delete unpublished changes"** to revert
- Or edit again and republish

## 👥 Getting Help

If you need help:
1. Contact your website administrator
2. Take a screenshot of any error messages
3. Describe what you were trying to do

## 🎨 Tips for Great Content

- **Images**: Use high-quality, professional photos
- **Variety**: Mix photos of events, people, and activities
- **Consistency**: Keep your branding consistent across all images
- **Accessibility**: Always add descriptive "Alt Text" to images
- **Social Links**: Keep them up to date!

Happy editing! 🎉
